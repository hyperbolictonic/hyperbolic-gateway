## Known limitations / follow-ups (not done here)
- **Aggregator truncation:** when Readability prunes a huge list page, the fallback converts the whole
  body; on Skyscanner that exceeds 25 000 chars and the tail is cut. A card-aware condenser
  (`[data-testid]` / `[role=listitem]` rows) would keep it compact — deliberately out of scope.
- **No `wait_for` primitive:** `isLoading` stays `true` on ad-heavy SPAs; harvests must be timed by
  the caller. `include_offscreen` + scroll partly mitigates.
- **`get_network_traffic`:** verbose headers and an ignored `url_pattern`; streamed bodies show
  `hasBody:false`.

## Design notes / guardrails
- **Context-light:** card harvest emits short condensed lines, not raw HTML.
- **Humanlike:** everything stays on the markdown → `click_mark` path; no JS mutation.
- **Idempotent:** `include_offscreen` is opt-in (defaults off) so existing behaviour is unchanged.
