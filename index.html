TIMELAPSE RECORDER

Records this Cyberdeck window. A frame is captured every interval and played back at the chosen frame rate, so the video plays faster than real time. At 1 fps capture and 30 fps playback, one minute of work becomes two seconds of video.

idle

While off, record_start, record_stop and list_recordings are not registered in tools/list and the bridge refuses calls with RECORDING_TOOLS_DISABLED. Only this panel can change it — no tool can flip it. An already-connected agent must reconnect to see the change.

Output directory

Finished files are `.webm` (VP9). list_recordings returns their absolute paths from this folder, ready to type into a file-upload field.
